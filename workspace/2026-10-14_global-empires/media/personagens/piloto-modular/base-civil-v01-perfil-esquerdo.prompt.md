## Prompt

Use case: precise-object-edit, multi-view 3D production reference.
Turn ONLY the camera of the supplied shared civilian character FRONT reference to a strict orthographic LEFT PROFILE (show his left side, nose pointing toward the LEFT edge of the image).
Output exactly ONE full-length character, not a sheet, no text or diagrams.

Preserve the EXACT SAME man, face identity, short dark wavy hair and stubble, body proportions, head-to-body ratio, shoulder and hip widths, limb thickness, hands, boot sizes and clothing geometry from the reference. This is the SAME 3D character rotated, not a new interpretation. Preserve the entire symmetric A-pose in 3D: arms straight about 30 degrees away from torso to the sides, hands empty and relaxed, feet parallel hip-width, no step or contrapposto. In profile the farther arm and leg may be occluded naturally; do not move the pose to make both visible. Show the near arm with enough silhouette clarity for reconstruction; no extra limb or mixed front/profile body parts.
Same ivory rolled-sleeve cotton shirt, exact collar, front placket, sleeve roll height just below elbow, tucked waistline, same brown-khaki moderately loose canvas trousers and dark brown ankle lace-up boots with thick soles. Preserve the cloth cut, actual thickness, broad fold placement and material boundaries. No hats, vest, belts, straps, tools, weapons, bags, scarves or new accessories. Anatomical realism with the same restrained RTS robustness.
Match the input's portrait 2:3 framing, character height within frame and crown/sole vertical positions. Level orthographic side camera, no 3/4, no perspective. Full head and full boots visible with clear margins, same uniform light warm-gray opaque background and even studio illumination, only minimal soft contact shadow. Neutral unbaked material colors. The four views will be submitted together to an image-to-3D generator, so exact consistent volume and proportions are more important than artistic variation.
