## Prompt

Use case: stylized-concept, 3D production reference.
EDIT the supplied original GLOBAL EMPIRES worker and militia concept sheets into ONE clean FRONT orthographic image of their SHARED CIVILIAN CLOTHING BASE, intended directly for image-to-3D generation. Image 1 U01 is the primary worker character/clothing inspiration; Image 2 U03 is the primary militia clothing inspiration. The output is ONE character, NOT a sheet or collage. Remove all titles, panels, diagrams, weapons, hat, vest, belts, bags, scarves and props. Show no environment.

SUBJECT: one adult Brazilian male rural industrial-era character, short dark brown hair, medium warm brown skin, short neatly shaped stubble, neutral expression, calm face, 7.2-head-tall believable adult proportions. This is a reusable medium-build human foundation for worker, militia and later era infantry. Broad but natural shoulders, healthy torso, strong forearms, ordinary waist, balanced leg length. Restrained RTS proportional emphasis: palms/fingers and practical boots around 8 percent more robust than photographic anatomy, never caricature, no enormous head, no short dwarf legs, no bodybuilder muscles. Keep head, neck, hands and limbs clearly formed with clean anatomical articulation. Hands natural and open with separated relaxed fingers, no fists. Arms in symmetrical A-pose about 30 degrees away from torso, BOTH arms straight and empty; palms angled toward thighs, feet hip-width parallel, no contrapposto. Subject is looking directly forward.

CLOTHING BASE: a plain warm ivory/off-white cotton work shirt with open short collar, simple visible front placket and sleeves neatly rolled to just below elbows, thick enough rolled cuffs to read at RTS distance. NO undershirt logos, harness, waistcoat, armor or decorative accessories. Shirt tucked cleanly into simple mid-brown khaki canvas trousers, moderately roomy at thighs and knees, controlled broad readable folds, modest taper at ankles. Plain dark brown leather lace-up ANKLE boots with thick readable soles, anatomically plausible construction; no tall gaiters or knee boots. Shirt has real garment thickness at collar, cuffs and placket, seam logic and physically credible fit. Trousers sit correctly at natural waist, no skinny or balloon proportions. Simplify micro-wrinkles and busy fabric mottling: broad form before microdetail. Shirt, trousers and boots are cleanly separated by material and shape. No vest or belt: these are separately modeled modules later.

CAMERA/OUTPUT: strict orthographic FRONT, level camera, no perspective or 3/4 angle; fully visible crown to soles with 8 percent clear margin around figure. Vertical portrait 2:3 composition, the character occupies about 86 percent of image height and is centered. Both soles on one horizontal ground datum near the bottom. Even neutral studio illumination, no dramatic rim lights, no painted lighting or AO grime, uniform very light warm-gray flat opaque background and only a minimal contact shadow. Detailed stylized-realistic game-character render matching the original project's art direction, modeled forms and readable material boundaries, no cartoon cel outline. Crisp silhouette suited to a 55-degree RTS camera when built in 3D. This front view will define exact anatomy and clothing for all subsequent views: make every feature intentional and reproducible. No text, no scale ruler, no floor island, no color swatches, no second person, no detached body parts.
