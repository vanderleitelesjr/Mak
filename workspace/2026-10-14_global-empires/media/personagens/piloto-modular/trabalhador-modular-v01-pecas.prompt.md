## Prompt

Use case: stylized-concept, modular equipment reference. Redesign the supplied assembled character into a clean isolated EQUIPMENT REFERENCE BOARD for modular 3D asset production. Preserve the actual equipment shapes, cut, colors, scale relationships and details from the assembled reference. The character's body is NOT in the output. Each equipment module is independently fully visible, surrounded by large clear gutters, no touching objects, no body parts/mannequin or connecting floor. A tidy landscape 3:2 board on plain very light warm-gray background with even neutral lighting, minimal shadows and dark forest-green Portuguese serif labels. Technical clarity, not beauty clutter. Every leather/cloth module must have believable thickness, seams and construction; no floating disconnected buckles or impossible cloth. Render modules at their correct relative physical scale with each other, with enough resolution for image-to-3D references. Large readable forms suitable for RTS; same art direction as input. Show the same item twice ONLY when explicitly requesting front/back or top/side. Each cell holds only that item and its short label. No rulers, statistics, colored grids, invented props, weapon schematics, extra variations or fancy ornamental frames. Do NOT add the civilian shirt, pants, boots or human. This is the modular equipment library layout, not a dressed character.
