The creature: Arachne, a master weaver frozen mid-metamorphosis by a goddess's curse — ONE continuous body, absolutely not a woman riding a spider. From the hips down her flesh flows into a spider's body through a deliberately designed transition: her skin hardens gradually into iridescent black-violet chitin in overlapping corset-like plates that visibly GROW out of her lower back and hips; her own human legs have already fused and lengthened into the front pair of spider legs, human muscle fading into segmented chitin along their length; six more legs arch from her lower back.
