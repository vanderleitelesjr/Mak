B — Armored oiran costume (~50% japan)
Nano Banana Pro edit
Prompts

Saved generation prompt

Keep this exact character — her face, violet skin, four amber eyes, white braided hair, proportions, pose, the big ornamented abdomen, the eight thick armored legs, and the bold hand-painted game-art style. Redesign ONLY her costume as a fusion of samurai armor and an oiran's kimono: a lacquered chest plate shaped like crossed kimono lapels, a wide gold OBI belt tied at the waist junction with a large structured knot at the front, short hanging plum-silk kimono sleeves on her arms that stay well above the legs, and her crown fused with a fan of golden kanzashi pins. The abdomen ornament becomes a golden spider-web KAMON with seigaiha waves. Palette: deep plum, lacquer black, gold. Nothing hangs over the legs, no fabric near the ground. Same backdrop, no text, no watermark.

C — Nihonga render language (style shift)
Nano Banana Pro edit
Prompts

Saved generation prompt

Keep this exact character and design — her face, violet skin, four amber eyes, white braided hair, crown, proportions, pose, the big ornamented abdomen, the eight thick armored legs. Change ONLY the rendering language: repaint the whole image in a NIHONGA and ukiyo-e influenced style — flatter elegant color planes, visible mineral-pigment brushwork, fine sumi-ink linework on the edges, flat gold-leaf accents catching light on the armor and the abdomen crest. Palette shifts to sumi black, deep plum, muted vermilion accents and gold leaf. The character stays a bold game-art boss in shape, but painted like a Japanese masterwork. Same backdrop, no text, no watermark.

D — Full reimagining (~70% japan)
Nano Banana Pro edit
Prompts

Saved generation prompt

Take this character as the starting point and REIMAGINE her as a Japanese spider empress — principally new, not a copy. Keep her essence: the powerful silhouette, violet skin, four amber eyes, white hair, the huge abdomen, the eight thick mighty legs, the confident boss presence, the hand-painted finish. Rebuild everything else in Japanese language: her crown becomes a golden PAGODA-tier crown with kanzashi tassels, her armor becomes layered lacquered plates cut like kimono folds with a gold obi knot at the waist junction, the abdomen becomes deep lacquer black with a giant gold spider-web kamon and wisteria-plum gradient at the edges, the leg plates get vermilion lacquer joints with gold edging, and one small paper chochin lantern hangs from a short chain under her front leg's knee like a temple lantern. Palette: lacquer black, plum-wisteria, vermilion, gold. Nothing hangs over the legs, no fabric near the ground. Same backdrop, no text, no watermark.
