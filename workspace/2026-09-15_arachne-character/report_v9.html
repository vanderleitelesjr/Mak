A — Geisha classic — black lead

Prompts

Saved generation prompt

Keep this character's exact FORM: the bold chunky armor-plate shapes, the proportions, the pose, the huge round abdomen with its spider-web crest, the eight thick powerful legs, the hand-painted game-art finish. REPLACE the entire color identity with a classic GEISHA palette — striking, high-contrast, black-red-white: 1) Her skin becomes porcelain WHITE with traditional geisha makeup — a soft red blush accent at the outer corners of her eyes and on the cheekbones, deep red lips, fine red liner; her four eyes stay, now amber-red. 2) Her hair becomes glossy BLACK in a sculpted geisha shimada updo with red-and-gold kanzashi pins and one red silk ribbon. 3) The armor plates become deep sumi BLACK lacquer with thin VERMILION RED edging; the abdomen becomes black lacquer carrying the spider-web crest in bright vermilion with a white outline; her top layers get white silk accents. 4) Gold remains only as a rare complementary spark on pins and buckles. 60% black, 25% white, 15% vermilion red.
