Epic creature concept art for a AAA video game, a senior creature concept artist's final presentation piece, stylized realism like an Unreal Engine 5 cinematic — not photoreal, not cartoon. ONE creature, full body visible, all eight legs readable with clear gaps between limbs, three-quarter view, imposing stance with weight and personality, not a stiff neutral pose. The creature: Arachne, a master weaver frozen mid-metamorphosis by a goddess's curse — ONE continuous body, absolutely not a woman riding a spider. From the hips down her flesh flows into a spider's body through a deliberately designed transition: her skin hardens gradually into iridescent black-violet chitin in overlapping corset-like plates that visibly GROW out of her lower back and hips; her own human legs have already fused and lengthened into the front pair of spider legs, human muscle fading into segmented chitin along their length; six more legs arch from her lower back. Her elongating fingers end in black needle tips trailing glowing silk threads to a torn loom-tapestry she drags behind her like a web train; on the left side of her face three small extra amber eyes are opening; her hair is half a formal Greek braid, half dissolved into fine silk strands. 60% black-violet chitin, 30% pale marble skin and ivory silk, 10% amber glow of eyes and thread. Dramatic warm key light with a cool rim light, faint ground haze, minimal mid-gray gradient studio backdrop keeping the silhouette clean, no environment scene, no text, no watermark. 16:9.
